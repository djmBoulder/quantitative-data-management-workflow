# Course Completion And Badge Policies

These policies govern the **Quantitative Data Management and Workflow for Social Scientists** microcredential.

Enrollment and the Canvas course are not yet available. These policies describe the planned enrolled course; see [course availability](../README.md#course-availability).

## Canvas Course

The enrolled course will be scaffolded in Canvas. Students enroll in Canvas, work through the module sequence there, submit assignments and the capstone, and receive instructor feedback and grades. The repository supplies the lessons, code labs, teaching data, and reference materials used in Canvas.

Upload requested scripts, memos, checklists, datasets, logs, and other assignment evidence to Canvas. Keep generated files in local working/output/log folders and out of GitHub. Do not submit private, restricted, confidential, identifiable respondent data, student records, or data whose terms prohibit redistribution.

## Self-Paced Completion Window

The completion window begins when a student registers for the course and ends **one week (seven calendar days) before CU's semester grade-submission deadline**. All required module assignments, the capstone, and any capstone revisions must be submitted by that course cutoff. The remaining week gives the instructor time to finish grading and submit student grades to the registrar.

The instructor uses the applicable semester deadline in CU Boulder's [Grade Processing Calendars](https://www.colorado.edu/registrar/faculty-staff/calendar/grading), subtracts seven calendar days, and posts the resulting course cutoff date and time in Canvas each semester. Students should use the posted Canvas deadline and submit early enough to receive feedback and use their revision opportunities.

## Software Pathway

Students complete graded work in one primary pathway: **Stata or R**. The **dual Stata/R pathway is optional**. Students who choose it submit comparable workflows in both languages. All students review the parallel approaches conceptually.

## Capstone Data Options

The capstone has two data options with the same competency standards and portfolio requirements:

- **Option A: course-provided synthetic data.** Use the assigned `synthetic_gss_like` files. No separate data proposal is required.
- **Option B: instructor-approved student-selected data.** Students may use publicly distributable data relevant to their research or methods coursework. Submit the [data proposal](../assignments/capstone-data-proposal.md) in Canvas and obtain approval before beginning the capstone with those data.

For Option B, the source must explicitly permit public redistribution and the proposed educational use. Record the source, exact version or release, terms, and any required attribution. Public availability alone does not establish permission. Public-domain data or data with a license that permits the proposed redistribution and use may qualify. Private, restricted, confidential, identifiable respondent data, student records, and data whose terms prohibit redistribution are not eligible. Instructor approval cannot waive these restrictions.

The proposal defines a manageable project, the instructor's access to the exact inputs, and the evidence for reshaping, appending, and merging. Provide links and descriptions with the proposal, not data uploads. After approval, submit permitted input copies with the capstone or use instructor-approved retrieval instructions for the exact versions. Keep student-selected data in a local project folder or the ignored `data/raw/` folder, and keep them out of both course repositories. Do not replace the synthetic teaching files. All Module 00–09 assignments and guided labs continue to use their assigned teaching data.

Module 06 assignment evidence may demonstrate reshaping when it is not part of the capstone. For Option B, Module 07 assignment evidence may demonstrate append and/or merge when the corresponding operation does not apply to the approved data. State why it does not apply and identify the exact Canvas submission, filename, and script section in the final memo. Applicable operations must be performed and checked in the capstone. The instructor reviews the cited module evidence against the same competency standards; missing or inadequate evidence must be corrected before completion. Option A still requires its append and merge in the capstone. All Module 00–09 assignments remain required for both options.

Confirm changes to approved sources, scope, or evidence locations with the instructor in Canvas before proceeding. The synthetic option remains available if a proposal is not approved or a project proves unsuitable. Proposal approval does not extend the semester cutoff or change the two-revision capstone limit. See the [full assignment](../assignments/capstone-data-management-portfolio.md).

## Required Badge Evidence

**Module assignments count as required badge evidence.** Students submit the assignments for Modules 00–09 in Canvas and complete the Module 10 capstone portfolio. Module assignments provide practice and feedback while also documenting the competencies required for the badge. Follow each assignment's evidence requirements and use its checklist before submitting.

## Capstone Revisions

Students may submit **two capstone revisions after the initial submission** (up to three submissions in total). Each revision responds to instructor feedback and includes a brief explanation of what changed. Submit revisions in Canvas within the self-paced completion window. Revision opportunities do not extend the semester cutoff.

## Peer Review

**No peer review is required.** Instructor feedback guides revision and competency assessment.

## Completion Record And Badge Issuance

Students submit the capstone through Canvas. The instructor reviews the required module evidence and evaluates the capstone using the [capstone rubric](../rubrics/capstone-rubric.md). A **passing capstone grade in Canvas** records badge completion once the required course evidence demonstrates the badge competencies.

The instructor then notifies the **micro registrar** to issue the badge. Capstone submission alone does not record completion: a passing grade is required. Canvas holds the assignment submissions, feedback, and grades. The instructor also submits student grades to the registrar by CU's semester deadline.
