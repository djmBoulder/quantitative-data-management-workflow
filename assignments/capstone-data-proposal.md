# Capstone Student-Selected Data Proposal

Use this template if you want to complete the capstone with publicly distributable data relevant to your own research or methods coursework. Submit it in Canvas and receive instructor approval **before beginning the capstone with those data**. Option A, the course-provided synthetic data, does not require a proposal.

Keep the proposal brief. Provide links and descriptions for review; do not upload data with the proposal. Read the [capstone assignment](capstone-data-management-portfolio.md) and [data policy](../syllabus/course-policies.md#capstone-data-options) first.
